How to compare finds before ordering

  1. Open the product page. Check the selected size and color, then find the original seller link if it is available. Confirm CSSBuy accepts that link before placing an order.
  2. Use measurements you already know. Measure a similar garment flat and compare it with the seller’s chart. For shoes, compare insole length in centimeters.
  3. Review your warehouse QC photos. Confirm that the label and color match your selection. Ask for a ruler photo if an important measurement is missing.
  4. Estimate the delivered cost. Add applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and destination charges. Check whether the shipping line uses actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a directory you can use to shortlist products and find their source listings before checking whether CSSBuy can order them.

Where do the two buttons lead?

Both open HipobuyFind. Open an individual product to review its source information and current options.

Can I use older QC photos to choose a size?

Use older photos for an initial comparison. Confirm measurements against the seller’s current chart and request photos of your own item when available.
